Help & FAQs

Q. How do I write a review?
Click on "Write a Review" in the navigation and fill in the form.

Q. How do I rate a product or service?
When writing a review, select the number of stars you want to give, and add your feedback in the text field.

Q. Can I upload images with my review?
Yes, you can upload images to support your review.

Q.
Reviews are stored locally, there is no permanent storage.

Q. Can I view individual review pages?
Currently, all reviews are displayed together; individual review pages are not supported.
